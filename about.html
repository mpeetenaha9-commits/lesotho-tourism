About Lesotho

The Kingdom in the Sky

Our Story

Lesotho is a small mountain kingdom found in the southern part of Africa. It is completely surrounded by South Africa. We are proud to be the only country in the world that is fully above 1,000 metres in altitude. This is why we are called the "Kingdom in the Sky".

Our website helps visitors find the best tourist destinations, accommodation and activities in Lesotho. We want everyone to enjoy our beautiful mountains, friendly people and rich Basotho culture.

Why Visit Lesotho?

  • Beautiful mountains and waterfalls
  • Traditional Basotho culture and food
  • Snow in winter (June to August)
  • Adventure sports like hiking and horse riding
  • Friendly and welcoming people
